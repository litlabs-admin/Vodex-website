## About

Genworks is a healthcare solutions provider that enhances access to quality medical care in India through innovative technology and services.

## Challenges

### Genworks faced several hurdles in their telemarketing strategy:

-   **Low Conversion Rates**: Traditional telemarketing methods delivered below-average outcomes, limiting the program's success.

-   **Customer Engagement Gaps**: Ensuring consistent and meaningful communication with potential customers was a major challenge.

-   **Missed Opportunities**: High disconnect rates during outreach led to the loss of potential leads.

-   **Scalability Challenges**: Expanding outreach efforts without increasing workforce costs was proving difficult.

### To address these challenges, Genworks needed a **scalable solution** to drive meaningful customer interactions and improve conversion rates.

## Solutions

### Vodex provided a tailored AI-powered telemarketing solution to transform Genworks’ outreach strategy:

-   **Optimized Follow-Up Strategy:** Automated follow-ups reconnected with disconnected customers, reducing missed opportunities. Ensured customers had a clear understanding of the **USG Scheme** benefits.

-   **Personalized Communication**: AI Voice Agents conducted tailored, empathetic conversations based on customer preferences and behavior Built trust and rapport, resulting in higher engagement levels  

-   ‍**Streamlined Outreach:** Automated the initial engagement process to promptly contact all leads and Improved response rates by efficiently handling customer inquiries.  

-   ‍**Scalable Operations**: Enabled Genworks to expand outreach to a broader audience without adding workforce costs. Ensured cost-effective scaling of outreach campaigns.

## Results

### The results achieved by Vodex’s AI-powered solution were **exceptional**:

-   **Enhanced Response Rate**: Achieved a **46.4% response rate**, exceeding industry benchmarks.

-   **Higher Conversion Rates**: Outperformed traditional telemarketing with a **5.7% conversion rate**, compared to the industry average of 2%.

-   **Reduced Missed Opportunities**: Automated follow-ups reduced missed customer connections by **30%**, ensuring better lead engagement.
