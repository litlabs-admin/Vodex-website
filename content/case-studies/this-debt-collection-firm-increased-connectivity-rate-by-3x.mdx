## About

They specialize in debt recovery services for Financial Institutions

## Challenges

## They faced challenges that disrupted smooth debt recovery operations.

-   **Inefficient Outreach:** Manual outreach limited the number of debtors they could contact, slowing down recovery.
-   **High Operational Costs**: Employing human agents for outreach was financially unsustainable and reduced profitability.
-   **Poor Debtor Engagement:** Inconsistent communication led to lower debtor response rates and a delay in overdue payments.
-   ‍**Low Call Connectivity:** A large number of calls went unanswered or diverted to voicemail, further hampering recovery efforts.

## Solutions

## Why They Chose Vodex

-   **AI Voice Agents for Automated Outreach**:  Enabled automated, scalable outreach to debtors, delivering timely reminders about overdue and upcoming payments.
-   ‍**Automated Redial Feature**: Enhanced call connectivity by automatically retrying calls to debtors who missed the first attempt.
-   **Cost Optimization**: By leveraging AI-driven automation, They significantly reduced operational costs while maintaining high outreach effectiveness.

## Results

## The implementation of Vodex AI brought measurable and **transformational results**:

-   **Enhanced Debtor Engagement**: AI Voice Agents ensured consistent and timely reminders, improving overall debtor engagement and communication.**‍**
-   **Higher Return on Investment (ROI)**: Debtor collection rates improved by **20%**, ensuring a significant ROI for them.
-   **Reduced Operational Costs:** Achieved a 70% reduction in operational costs through AI-powered outreach.
