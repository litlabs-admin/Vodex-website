This website uses cookies to store or retrieve information on your browser, which may include details about you, your preferences, or your device. Cookies are essential for the website to function properly and can provide a more personalized browsing experience tailored to your needs. While this information typically does not directly identify you, it enhances how the site operates. We respect your privacy and offer you the ability to opt out of non-essential cookies at any time. Please note, blocking certain cookies may affect your experience and limit the services we can provide. Below you fill find details on our cookie management policy

## Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be turned off. They enable basic features such as page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. Without these cookies, the website may not work properly. They are typically triggered by actions you take, such as adjusting privacy settings, logging in, or submitting forms. Although you can configure your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, doing so may interfere with the proper functioning of some site features. Rest assured, these cookies do not collect any personally identifiable information.

## Targeting Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver personalized advertising based on your interests. They maybe set by us or our advertising partners. These cookies help track your activity across websites to provide more relevant ads. They are used by these companies to create a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other websites. These cookies do not directly store personal information but rely on uniquely identifying your browser and device. If you choose not to allow these cookies, you may see fewer targeted ads. Our advertising partners may use these cookies to build a profile of your interests and display tailored ads on other websites. Although they do not collect personal information directly, they function by uniquely identifying your browser and device. If you decline these cookies, the ads you see may be less targeted.

## Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our site by collecting and reporting anonymous information. They allow us to measure and improve the performance of our site. They provide insights into which pages are most and least popular and how users navigate the site. The data collected by these cookies is aggregated and anonymized. If you disable these cookies, we won’t be able to track your visit or monitor the site’s performance.

## Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to offer enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services are integrated into our pages. If you disable these cookies, some or all of these services may not work properly.
